The ten-card rummy of Kosovo

Zhol, now on your phone. Open a table, share the four-letter code, and play with your friends in real time. Two to four players, no sign-up, and no real money.

How it plays

  • Ten cards each. Draw from the deck or the discard pile, build sets and runs, and use jokers to fill the gaps.
  • Open with 40. Your first lay-down has to be worth at least 40 points.
  • Go for Zhol. Lay down your whole hand in one turn and every other player's penalty doubles.
  • Last one standing. Penalty points add up round after round. Reach 101 and you are out; the last player left takes the pot.

Free chips, no real money

Everyone starts with 10,000 free chips and can top back up for free. Chips cannot be bought, have no cash value and cannot be cashed out. Kosova Zhol is intended for adults and offers no real-money gambling and no chance to win money or prizes.

Privacy

No accounts to create, no email address, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. The privacy policy lists the little that is stored and why.
