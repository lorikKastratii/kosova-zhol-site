Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version. Kosova Zhol does not ask who you are. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. The game keeps the name you type in, your chip balance, and a random identifier that ties your phone to that balance. There are no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising identifiers and no ads, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

Who this is from

Kosova Zhol is an iOS app made by BK Solutions SH.P.K. Questions about this policy, or about data held about you, go to contact@bk-solutions.info.

What the game stores

On our game server

  • Your display name — whatever you type in the lobby. Other players at your table see it next to your seat.
  • Your chip balance. Chips are virtual, free, and have no cash value.
  • A random account identifier and a secret token. These are generated when you first open the game and contain nothing about you. The token is how the server recognises your phone so your balance carries over between sessions.
  • What happens at your table while it is open — seats, cards, melds, discards, scores and the preset chat phrases sent. This is needed to run the game and is discarded when the table closes. The server's operational log notes when a name creates, joins or leaves a table, and is used only to keep the game running and fix problems.

On your phone

The secret token is kept in the iOS Keychain so you keep your chips between launches.

What is not collected

No email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, payment details, advertising identifier or device fingerprint. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and nothing tracks you across other apps or websites.

Who else sees it

  • Other players at your table see your display name, your score, the cards you lay down or discard, and the cards left in your hand when a round ends.
  • Our hosting provider. Game traffic passes through the service that hosts and connects to our game server (currently ngrok). Like any network service it handles connection details such as your IP address in order to deliver the traffic. We do not store IP addresses.

Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

How long it is kept

Your account identifier, display name and chip balance are kept for as long as the account exists, so your chips are there when you come back. Table data is discarded when the table closes.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the token from your phone, after which nobody can use that account again. To have the account removed from our server as well, email contact@bk-solutions.info with the display name you used and roughly when you last played, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Age

Kosova Zhol is played for a pot of virtual chips and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If what the game stores changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
